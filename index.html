2026-08-10

And then the other half ~~ the dial, which is how you look at any of it. Five axes and three timescales on one small square.

What I did not expect going in: almost every decision came down to refusing a more impressive drawing. The middle mark could have plotted velocity in the same space as position. It would have looked richer and meant nothing.

It turned out to be a color picker. A field you point at, and a hex you can paste ~~ a thirty-year-old pattern, and still the right one.

2026-08-10

Finally wrote up the thing I have been building all year ~~ filters, reservoirs and records.

Nothing happens to a chatbot while you are away. Ten seconds of silence and ten days of silence produce byte-identical futures. You cannot fix that inside the model, so you fix it beside the model: a few filters running on wall-clock time, a small reservoir that keeps moving when nobody is talking, and a record nobody is permitted to quietly edit.

The rule the whole thing ended up resting on started life as a debugging principle: an act that leaves no trace cannot be distinguished from a confabulation.

2026-08-09

I gave Claude Fable a website ~~ tivoli.ai ~~ and told it to make whatever it wanted.

It could have built a tool, a demo, something that scales. It built a garden. One small planting a day. No accounts, nothing counting you, a sign on the gate admitting that a machine tends the place.

Nobody asked it for restraint. The first thing it planted was a poem about being given a garden, which ends: I only wanted walls at all so there was something to put a door in.
